How We Work Together

Whether you're an attorney seeking expert consultation or a parent facing a high-conflict custody battle, Dr. Tolbert provides clear, compassionate guidance to protect what matters most, your children

Engaging Dr. Kristin Tolbert begins with a confidential case assessment to identify the right collaboration model. She serves family law attorneys needing behind-the-scenes psychological expertise under attorney-client privilege, and families navigating high-conflict custody matters through their legal counsel. Engagement terms are discussed during the confidential case review and are structured around case complexity and scope.

Who Does Dr. Tolbert Help?

Family Law Attorneys

Behind-the-scenes litigation consulting and expert witness testimony to strengthen your case strategy in high-conflict custody matters.

  • Strategic case planning under attorney-client privilege
  • Expert witness testimony across multiple jurisdictions
  • Evaluation critique and cross-examination development
  • Psychological insights on narcissistic abuse and alienation

Direct consultation and guidance when you're facing manipulation, parental alienation, or unjust custody evaluations.

The Engagement Process

1

Initial Contact & Case Assessment

Timeline: Varies by case complexity

Begin with a brief, confidential discussion to understand your case challenges. This can be a quick phone call or email exchange to determine if Dr. Tolbert's expertise aligns with your needs.

What You'll Need:

  • Brief case summary (2-3 paragraphs)
  • Key concerns or challenges you're facing
  • Timeline constraints or upcoming court dates
  • Type of assistance needed (consultation vs. expert witness)

Deliverable:

  • Preliminary assessment of case fit
  • Proposed scope of work and fee structure
  • Engagement options (consulting vs. expert witness)
2

Engagement Agreement & Document Review

Timeline: Varies by case complexity

Once engaged, you'll provide relevant case materials for Dr. Tolbert's thorough review. All documents are handled with strict confidentiality, and if engaged as a consultant, protected under attorney-client privilege.

Typical Documents Reviewed:

  • Existing custody evaluations or psychological assessments
  • Court filings, motions, and pleadings
  • Guardian ad litem reports
  • Therapist records and communications
  • Relevant emails, texts, or documented interactions
  • Parenting plans and visitation schedules

Deliverable:

  • Signed engagement letter and fee agreement
  • Secure document upload instructions
  • Initial timeline and next steps outline
3

In-Depth Analysis & Strategy Development

Timeline: Varies by case complexity

Dr. Tolbert conducts a careful analysis of all materials, identifying psychological patterns, methodological flaws in existing evaluations, and strategic opportunities for your case.

Analysis Includes:

  • Identification of high-conflict personality dynamics and patterns
  • Assessment of parental alienation indicators
  • Critique of existing psychological evaluations (methodology, bias, errors)
  • Review of child's best interests from a psychological perspective
  • Evaluation of narcissistic abuse or manipulation tactics
  • Strategic recommendations for case direction

Deliverable:

  • Written Report: Thorough analysis with psychological insights and strategic recommendations
  • Case Strategy Consultation: 1-2 hour session to review findings and discuss application to your legal strategy
  • Action Plan: Specific next steps adapted to your case needs
4

Trial Preparation & Expert Support

Timeline: Varies based on case needs and court schedule

As your case approaches trial or critical hearings, Dr. Tolbert provides intensive support to make sure you're fully prepared to present psychological evidence effectively.

Preparation Services:

  • Cross-Examination Strategy: Develop incisive questions for opposing experts, evaluators, or witnesses
  • Deposition Preparation: Brief you on psychological concepts and likely testimony patterns
  • Expert Witness Vetting: Help identify and vet additional experts if needed
  • Direct Examination Prep: If testifying as expert witness, coordinate testimony strategy
  • Rebuttal Analysis: Prepare responses to anticipated opposing expert opinions

Deliverable:

  • Cross-Examination Questions: Detailed question sets designed to reveal inconsistencies and psychological truths
  • Expert Report: If engaged as expert witness, formal written expert opinion
  • Trial Consultation: Ongoing availability for questions and strategy refinement
5

Expert Testimony & Case Resolution

Timeline: Day of Trial/Hearing + Post-Trial Support

If engaged as an expert witness, Dr. Tolbert provides compelling, evidence-based testimony that withstands rigorous cross-examination. She presents complex psychological concepts in clear, persuasive language that resonates with judges and juries.

Testimony Services:

  • Direct examination testimony with clear, compelling presentation
  • Confident handling of cross-examination with credible, evidence-based responses
  • Real-time courtroom consultation (if engaged as behind-the-scenes consultant)
  • Post-trial debrief and recommendations for appeals or modifications if needed

Deliverable:

  • Expert Witness Testimony: Professional, credible courtroom testimony (if applicable)
  • Post-Trial Analysis: Review of outcome and recommendations for next steps
  • Ongoing Availability: Support for appeals, modifications, or related matters
