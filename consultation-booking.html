How Engagements Work

All engagements begin with a confidential case review to assess the psychological dynamics, identify key issues, and determine how Dr. Tolbert can most effectively support your litigation strategy.

Engagement terms are discussed during this initial review and are structured around case complexity, scope of work, and timeline. Dr. Tolbert works on a monthly retainer basis with invoices issued to the retaining attorney's firm.

Retainer Structure

  • Monthly retainer agreements
  • Invoices issued to attorney's firm
  • ACH or wire transfer payment
  • Detailed time tracking and reporting
  • Flexible engagement terms based on case needs

Privilege Protection

  • All work product protected under attorney-client privilege
  • Confidential case materials handling
  • Non-discoverable consulting services
  • Secure communication protocols
  • Professional liability coverage maintained
