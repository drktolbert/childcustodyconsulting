Attorney Collaboration

Smooth Integration With Your Legal Team

Dr. Tolbert doesn't just provide expert opinions. She becomes a strategic partner in your litigation, working under attorney-client privilege to strengthen every aspect of your case.

Dr. Kristin Tolbert collaborates with family law attorneys in two distinct roles: as a behind-the-scenes litigation consultant working under attorney-client privilege, and as a court-qualified expert witness providing courtroom testimony. She is available for litigation consultation nationwide and internationally, and provides expert witness testimony in jurisdictions that admit her credentials. All strategic work as a litigation consultant is confidential and non-discoverable.

Collaboration Models

How We Work Together

Litigation Consultant (Attorney-Client Privilege)

Dr. Tolbert is retained by your firm as part of the legal team. All work product, communications, and strategic discussions are protected and non-discoverable.

Services Include:

  • Thorough evaluation critique and analysis
  • Case strategy development from psychological perspective
  • Cross-examination question preparation
  • Expert witness vetting and selection
  • Deposition preparation and support
  • Trial preparation and strategy sessions
  • Identification of narcissistic abuse patterns
  • Analysis of parental alienation dynamics
Protected Communication: As part of your legal team, all consultations, emails, reports, and strategic discussions are privileged and not subject to discovery.

Dr. Tolbert provides qualified expert testimony in court proceedings across multiple jurisdictions, delivering credible, persuasive testimony that judges trust.

Testimony Types:

Collaboration in Practice

What Do Attorneys Experience Working with Dr. Tolbert?

Evaluation Critique

When an attorney engages Dr. Tolbert to review an opposing expert's custody evaluation, she conducts a systematic methodological analysis, identifying bias, unsupported conclusions, and deviations from professional standards. This analysis directly informs cross-examination strategy and, when applicable, rebuttal testimony.

Parental Alienation Cases

In cases involving alleged parental alienation, Dr. Tolbert helps attorneys understand the clinical indicators, identify how alienating behaviors manifest in communications and parenting interactions, and develop a case strategy that clearly presents these dynamics to the court in terms judges can evaluate.
