How Litigation Consultants Strengthen Cases Without Testifying

Many attorneys don't realize that some of the most powerful expert work happens behind the scenes, protected by attorney-client privilege.

The Consulting Advantage

Litigation consultants provide all the psychological expertise of expert witnesses but with critical strategic advantages: confidentiality, flexibility, and cost-effectiveness.

  • All analysis protected under attorney-client privilege
  • No discovery obligations. Opposing counsel never sees consultant work
  • No deposition exposure
  • Freedom to explore multiple theories without commitment
  • Ability to pivot strategy based on consultant feedback

When Consulting is More Effective Than Testimony

Not every case needs expert testimony. Sometimes consulting provides everything you need at fraction of the cost.

  • When you need evaluation critique to develop cross-examination
  • When psychological insights inform your case strategy but don't require testimony
  • When you want to explore whether expert testimony would help before committing
  • When settlement is likely after strong deposition performance
  • When you need ongoing strategic guidance throughout litigation

How Consultants Win Cases

Consultants shape outcomes through strategic influence rather than courtroom testimony.

  • Devastating cross-examination questions that force opposing experts into damaging admissions
  • Case strategy grounded in psychological reality rather than assumptions
  • Early identification of winning arguments and dead ends
  • Settlement leverage created by exposing evidentiary weaknesses
  • Protection of children through better understanding of psychological harm
