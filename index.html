Frequently Asked Questions

What is a psychology litigation consultant?

A psychology litigation consultant is a licensed psychologist who works behind the scenes with attorneys to analyze psychological evidence, critique custody evaluations, develop cross-examination strategies, and identify credible expert witnesses. All work is protected under attorney-client privilege. Learn more about the role

How does Dr. Tolbert help family law attorneys?

Dr. Tolbert integrates into legal teams to provide strategic psychological expertise in high-conflict custody cases. This includes reviewing and critiquing custody evaluations, developing targeted cross-examination questions, identifying patterns of narcissistic abuse or parental alienation, and helping attorneys understand complex psychological dynamics. View services for attorneys

Where does Dr. Tolbert provide expert witness testimony and consultation?

Dr. Tolbert is a Florida Licensed Psychologist who has been qualified as an expert witness in state court proceedings and provides expert testimony in jurisdictions that admit her credentials. She offers behind-the-scenes litigation consultation to family law attorneys throughout the United States and internationally, with all remote work conducted via phone and secure video conference.

How are engagements structured?

Engagement terms are discussed during a confidential case review and are structured around case complexity and scope. Initial case reviews are available by contacting Dr. Tolbert's office at 561-429-2140 or DrTolbert@ChildCustodyConsulting.com. All attorney consultations are protected under attorney-client privilege.

Does Dr. Tolbert understand Florida custody law?

Yes. As a Florida Licensed Psychologist, Dr. Tolbert has extensive expertise in Florida custody law, particularly Florida Statute §61.13 (Best Interests of the Child) and Florida Statute §61.20 (Social Investigations). She applies these statutory factors to her psychological analysis and helps attorneys align psychological evidence with Florida's legal requirements for parental responsibility and time-sharing determinations.
